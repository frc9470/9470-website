How Our Sponsorship is Organized

We sincerely appreciate your interest and gratitude in supporting our robotics team. We continuously race to raise funds for our team, especially as a younger team with lots of purchases necessary to give us the advantage many other teams have. Your generous donation will be instrumental in not only building our competition-grade robot but also in providing our club members with hands-on educational experiences. Each contribution directly supports the funding of critical components, software, and entry into competitions.

We offer four tiers of sponsorship, each corresponding to a different donation amount, with varying benefits for your company:
