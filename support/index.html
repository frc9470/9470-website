Team 9470

Support Us

Why You Should Support Us:

We are a rookie STEM team that needs funds to create the robot which we will use in the competition. Funding is an important part of building a robot, especially for high-stakes competitions like FRC. We come to the competition to win, and this competition means a lot to us. The FRC competition is an exhilarating experience for our members, fostering creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving skills. However, it also demands substantial resources. With budgets often exceeding $30,000 (and some teams reaching six digits), every dollar is put to good use. Currently, our team operates with a budget ranging from $10,000 to $15,000. While we are grateful for the support we receive from our school and parents, this budget places limitations on our performance potential. If you were to support us, your contribution would be greatly appreciated and we will provide you with our sponsor benefits.
